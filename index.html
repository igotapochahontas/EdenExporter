EdenExporter

Early Earth

A three-dimensional oscilloscope for modeling — virtual-reality organic life, grown from sound. Pick a source, then Begin: the demo track is baked right into the page and starts instantly, or choose the mic and let the visualizer dance to whatever is playing in the room. You can load your own song any time once you're in.

Tip: drag & drop any audio file onto the page to play your own song.

📱 iPhone tip: the silent switch on the side of your phone mutes this app — flip it off and turn the volume up to hear the music.
